# Balance sweep report

Generated by `tools/SiJenggo.Balance` (see [BALANCE.md](BALANCE.md)). Players are driven either by the
**skilled bot** (`SkilledBot`: steps out of hazards, dodges about 60% of the attacks it can react to after a
12-tick delay) or by the **in-game companion AI** (`PlayerLoadout.IsAi`: never dodges, blocks or avoids hazards).
All fighters use base stats (no levels, skills or gear) and 3 lives; Deaths counts every life lost, so a loss
is 4 per player. The co-op partner is the next character in roster order (Jenggo+Bima, Bima+Rani, Rani+Ucup,
Ucup+Jenggo). Bots are not humans: read these numbers as relative signals, not as the experience a player will have.

- Sessions: 2688 (8 seeds per configuration), 49.7 simulated hours
- Wall time: 7.9 s on 8 threads
- Caps: boss arena 600 s, full chapter 1500 s (a capped run counts as a loss in Win%)
- Times are medians in seconds over the runs that reached that point; fight time and phase times only count wins.
- Damage is total damage taken by all players per run (mean); the share columns split it by source:
  the boss itself, other enemies (minions, shadow clones, wave enemies), and hazards (boss mechanics, phase shockwaves, ambient).
